What It Does

👻

Always On Top & Click-Through

Floats cleanly over your apps without blocking buttons behind it, and softly fades when your cursor passes over.

⌨️

Types & Clicks Along Globally

Mirrors your real typing, clicks, scroll wheel, and cursor gaze across all apps — with optional Privacy Mode to hide key names.

🎨

Custom Coats & JSON Sharing

Edit any of the 15 coats into your own named designs, right-click to rename or tweak, and copy/paste JSON to share cats.

📐

Position, Multi-Monitor & Scale

Snap to any corner or monitor, drag directly in Drag Mode, scale from 40% to 200%, and tune opacity to taste.

📥

Quiet System Tray Companion

Zero taskbar clutter — close Settings to hide it to the tray, toggle Drag Mode anytime, or set it to start with Windows.

✨

Seamless In-App Updates

Automatically checks for new releases and installs updates silently in place without re-running the installer wizard.
